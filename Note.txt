2. Original implementation using paired data for model selection, I used kid/fid
3. Batch size affect GAN loss, try with moderate batch_size between 32-64. tested batch_size 64 better than 128
4. taking median of LUT weights reduce noise. median works better than mean
5. larger batch size -> smoother gradient -> increase learning rate -> instability 
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TibQO_xv1zc&t=1982s, 

6. tunning lambda_smooth (parameters to increase the penaty for total variance) does not remove the pixelation artefacts. 
In fact increasing and decreasing the lambda_smooth can introduce more artefacts if the gan loss get crazy.
